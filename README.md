# Project-Firepower
A Roblox require-style script with one main selling point; Guns. Big, floating guns. And YOU get to control all of them!

Made by v05n, with the help of @VikkoMakesStuff

## Introduction

Project: Firepower spawns ‎you with the following weapons:
- 2 Rocket Launchers
- 2 Railguns
- 2 Laser Guns
- 1 Plasma Cannon
- And 4 primary weapons that can be switched to machine guns, shotguns, and gatling guns.
- All of these weapons will aim towards your cursor position at all times.

You'll also spawn with a hovering platform that you stand on, which generates a protective forcefield around you.

## Stats

MaxHealth: 200  
Walkspeed: 60  
Jumppower: 70  
Ammo: 1,000

## Moveset

### ‎M1: Raw Firepower  
‎While held, the user points their hand towards their cursor position and starts firing their primary weapons, using up ammo in the process.  
This move varies depending on what primary the user has. Jump power is reduced to 60 while firing.  
The user can use most moves while this move is active.  
If the user is Overclocked, then all primary weapons will fire twice as fast.

* ‎Type 1: Machine Guns  
‎Consistency is key. Shoots out heavy-hitting bullets with a decent firerate.  
Walk speed is reduced to 50 while firing, and uses up 5 ammo per shot.  
Damage: 3 per bullet  
‎Firerate: 0.05s

* Type 2: Shotguns  
Get the job done quick. Shoots out 6 pellets with slight spread. High burst damage, but comes with a slower firerate.  
Unlike the other primaries, all 4 shotguns fire at the same time.  
Walk speed is reduced to 55 while firing, and uses up 25 ammo per shot. (for each shotgun btw)  
Damage: 1.5 per pellet  
‎Firerate: 0.5s
‎
* ‎Type 3: Gatling Guns  
‎Lay waste to them all. Shoots out smaller, less powerful bullets with slight spread, but has a much faster firerate, and also speeds up as the user keeps firing.  
Walk speed is reduced to 45 while firing, and uses up 1 ammo per shot.
(no these gatling guns DO NOT have to rev up before firing we arent going with this trope)  
‎Damage: 1 per bullet  
‎Firerate: Starts at 0.03s, exponentially speeds up to 0.01s minimum


### Q: Warp  
‎The user instantly teleports 50 studs from their original position, directed by the direction they're moving.  
‎Cooldown: 2s
‎

### ‎E: Shock-nade  
‎The user pulls out and throws a grenade towards the cursor position that stuns nearby enemies upon exploding.  
‎Damage: 25  
‎Cooldown: 10s


### R: Recharge  
‎While held, the user slows down to charge up energy, regaining 5 ammo every 0.015 seconds.  
This move stops once the user, either stops holding, or fully recharges their ammo.    
‎If the user is Overclocked, they regain 5 ammo every 0.01 seconds instead.  
‎Cooldown: 5s


### F: Primary Switch  
‎The user emits a flash of light, switching their primary to the next type.  
‎Cooldown: 0.5s
‎

### G: Reconstruction  
‎The user slows down to bring up a panel in front of them. Then they start tapping the panel to reconstruct their forcefield, healing back 20% of their maximum health.  
Cannot be used while firing or Overclocked.  
‎Cooldown: 40s
‎

### Z: Bombardment  
‎A beeping noise will play, then the user fires every missile from their missile launchers one by one. These missiles explode upon contact with any surface, and have homing capabilities. Allowing them to follow the user's cursor position, albeit with some slight inaccuracy. They'll also explode 10 seconds after being fired.  
‎Damage: 10 per hit  
‎Cooldown: 14s
‎

### X: Piercing Shots  
‎The user briefly winds up and fires their railguns all at once, shooting out beams that can pierce multiple targets.  
‎Damage: 35 per hit  
‎Cooldown: 10s
‎

### ‎C: Concentrated Rays  
‎The user begins powering up their laser guns. These laser guns focus onto the user's cursor position and fire a constant ray of light that melts anything in it's path, lasting for 5 seconds.  
‎Damage: 1 every tick  
‎Cooldown: 25s
‎

### V: Plasmatic Blast  
‎The user charges up and fires their plasma cannon, shooting out a slow-moving, but deadly plasma projectile. This projectile exponentially speeds up and creates a large explosion upon impact, and also deals constant damage to nearby enemies as it travels.  
‎Damage (Projectile): 0.5 + 0.02 for every tick (Explosion): 100  
‎Cooldown: 35s
‎

### ‎B: OVERCLOCK!!!   
‎YOU HAVE 40 SECONDS. GO BALLISTIC. DESTROY THEM ALL. DESTROY THEM ALL!  
The user breaks their forcefield to buff themselves greatly. While Overclocked, all moves will deal 1.5x more damage and gain lifesteal, but the user will start taking damage over time. They wont take any more damage over time once their health reaches below 1/10th of their maximum health.  
‎Unlike other moves, this one requires the user to deal enough damage in order for them to use it.  
‎Damage Requirement: 2,000
